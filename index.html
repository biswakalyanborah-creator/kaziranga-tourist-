🌲 Kaziranga National Park Ranges

Explore the four majestic tourism zones of the sanctuary:

1. Central Range (Kohora)

The primary tourist hub featuring vast grasslands and wetlands. Perfect for spotting rhinos, elephants, wild boars, and swamp deer during jeep safaris.

2. Western Range (Bagori)

Known for marshy tracts and rich aquatic plants. Excellent spot for observing wild water buffaloes and close encounters with rhinos.

3. Eastern Range (Agoratoli)

A tranquil deep-forest area ideal for bird watchers, pelicans, migratory waterfowl, and serene landscape photography.

4. Burapahar Range

Characterized by hilly terrains, dense tropical forests, and panoramic views along the Brahmaputra River banks.
