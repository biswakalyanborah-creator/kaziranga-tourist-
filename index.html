🌲 Kaziranga Tourist Places & Ranges

1. Central Range (Kohora)

The prime zone for jeep safaris. Known for high concentrations of One-Horned Rhinoceros, wild elephants, and swamp deer against a backdrop of beautiful grasslands.

2. Western Range (Bagori)

Famous for close-up sightings of rhinos and water buffaloes. Features picturesque water bodies and rich birdwatching spots.

3. Eastern Range (Agoratoli)

Best for bird lovers and wild water buffaloes. It offers deep wilderness, wetlands, and a quiet, peaceful safari experience.

4. Burapahar Range

Hilly terrain ideal for trekking and adventurous jeep rides, offering magnificent views of the Brahmaputra river valleys.
