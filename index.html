🌲 Kaziranga Scenery & Tourist Ranges

Immerse yourself in the breathtaking landscapes, lush wet grasslands, and dense tropical forests of Kaziranga National Park — home to the world's largest population of the Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros.

Central Range (Kohora)

High density of rhinos, elephants, and grassland panoramas. Main hub for jeep safaris.

Western Range (Bagori)

Wetlands and water bodies offering majestic sightings of rhinos and wild water buffaloes.

Eastern Range (Agoratoli)

A paradise for bird watchers, swamp deer, and peaceful deep-forest trails.

Burapahar Range

Hilly terrains, dense canopies, and scenic views along the Brahmaputra river.
