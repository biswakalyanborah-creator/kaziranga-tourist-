🌲 Kaziranga Tourist Ranges & Explanation

Explore the four major ranges of Kaziranga National Park:

1. Central Range (Kohora)

The core tourist hub featuring vast grasslands and water bodies. Best place for sighting the Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros, elephants, and swamp deer during jeep safaris.

2. Western Range (Bagori)

Known for marshy wetlands and rich aquatic vegetation. Excellent spot for observing wild water buffaloes and rhinos closely near water channels.

3. Eastern Range (Agoratoli)

A serene, peaceful deep-forest area ideal for bird watching, migratory birds, pelicans, and undisturbed wildlife photography.

4. Burapahar Range

Characterized by hilly terrains, dense tropical woods, and beautiful viewpoints along the Brahmaputra River.
