🌲 Kaziranga Safari Ranges

1. Central Range (Kohora): Core zone with grasslands, best for One-Horned Rhino and elephant sightings.

2. Western Range (Bagori): Marshy wetlands, ideal for wild water buffaloes and close rhino views.

3. Eastern Range (Agoratoli): Deep forest area, best for bird watchers and photography.

4. Burapahar Range: Hilly landscape along the Brahmaputra River.
